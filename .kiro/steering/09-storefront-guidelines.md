# Storefront Guidelines

## Header

Premium compact navigation. Desktop can include logo, Home, Shop, Categories, Brands, New Arrivals/Sale, search, account, wishlist and cart. Mobile must collapse intelligently.

## Home

Use an editorial composition rather than a wall of products: hero → service/trust highlights → categories → featured products → new arrivals/editorial promotion → more collections → footer. Optional sections should disappear cleanly when data is absent.

## Catalog

Support breadcrumb, title, description, product count, search, sort, filters, product grid and pagination. Desktop can use a filter sidebar; mobile should use a filter drawer.

## Product cards

Image-first. Show name, price, compare-at/discount where applicable, useful stock state, wishlist and add-to-cart without clutter.

## Product detail

Large gallery plus focused purchase information: title, price, discount, variants, availability, quantity, add-to-cart, buy-now where supported, delivery estimate and details. Follow with description, shipping, returns, reviews where supported and related products.

## Cart and checkout

Cart drawer for quick access and dedicated cart page for full management. Checkout should be focused and trustworthy with address, delivery, payment and an authoritative order summary including subtotal, discount, tax, shipping, COD and total.

## Footer

Render store name, address, email, phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and policy links only when configured. Never show empty placeholders.

Policies such as terms, privacy, shipping, refund, cancellation and return must come from backend configuration.
